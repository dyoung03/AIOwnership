AI Ownership Assessment

When you make something with AI, it often feels like yours. Sometimes that feeling is earned and sometimes it isn't.

This activity will take about four minutes. You'll give a gut reaction first, then go through the work part by part to identify the work you did, and the work the AI did. At the end you'll see how your instinct compares with your own account of what you did.

Nothing is scored and nothing is sent anywhere. This is an activity to build awareness and reflection. Answer honestly as you reflect on your work.

The three routes to ownership

Something feels like ours through control over it, knowledge of why it is the way it is, and what we invested in it. AI tends to leave one of these much thinner than the others.
